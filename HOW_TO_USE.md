## 1️⃣ ORDER TRACKING (NEW!)

### How to Track Your Orders:

#### Method 1: From Navbar
```
1. Look at the top navbar
2. Find "Track Your Orders" section
3. Click on it
4. Dashboard opens with all your orders
5. Click any order to see tracking timeline
```

#### Method 2: From User Menu
```
1. Click on "Hello, [Your Name]" in navbar
2. Dropdown menu appears
3. Click "📊 Dashboard" or "📦 Your Orders"
4. View all orders
5. Click any order for tracking
```

#### What You'll See:
- ✅ **5-Stage Timeline:**
  - 📝 Processing
  - ✅ Confirmed
  - 📦 Shipped
  - 🚚 Out for Delivery
  - 🎉 Delivered

- ✅ **Order Details:**
  - Order ID
  - Order Date
  - Estimated Delivery
  - Total Amount
  - Payment Status

- ✅ **Delivery Information:**
  - Full address
  - Map view (click "View on Map")
  - Delivery location

- ✅ **Actions:**
  - Download Invoice
  - Contact Support
  - Cancel Order (if not delivered)

## 2️⃣ DELIVERY OPTIONS (NEW!)

### How to Select Delivery:

```
1. Add items to cart
2. Click "Proceed to Checkout"
3. Fill your shipping address
4. See 4 delivery options:
```

#### Available Options:

**🚚 Standard Delivery**
- Time: 5-7 days
- Cost: FREE
- Best for: Regular orders

**⚡ Express Delivery**
- Time: 2-3 days
- Cost: ₹99
- Best for: Quick delivery

**🏃 Same Day Delivery**
- Time: Today
- Cost: ₹199
- Best for: Urgent orders

**📅 Scheduled Delivery**
- Time: Choose your date
- Cost: ₹149
- Best for: Planned delivery

### How It Works:
```
1. Click on any delivery option
2. Option gets highlighted in blue
3. Price automatically added to total
4. See updated total at bottom
5. Continue to payment
6. Your choice is saved with order
```

## 4️⃣ PRODUCT COMPARISON (NEW!)

### How to Compare Products:

#### Step 1: Add Products to Comparison
```
1. Browse any product
2. Look for balance scale icon (⚖️) at top-left
3. Click it to add to comparison
4. Icon turns blue when added
5. Add up to 4 products
```

#### Step 2: View Comparison
```
1. See counter at top: "X in comparison"
2. Blue bar appears with "Compare Now" button
3. Click "Compare Now"
4. See side-by-side comparison
```

#### What You Can Compare:
- ✅ Product images
- ✅ Names
- ✅ Prices
- ✅ Ratings
- ✅ Reviews count
- ✅ Features
- ✅ Specifications

#### Remove from Comparison:
```
1. Click balance scale icon again
2. Or remove from comparison modal
```
